General information only

Morrow is an educational calculator for illustrative mortgage balance, overpayment and loan-to-value scenarios. It does not provide mortgage advice, recommend a particular mortgage product, compare live rates, assess suitability, arrange a mortgage or guarantee savings, eligibility or future property values.

Estimates can differ

The model uses monthly interest based on the entered rate and assumes the rate and payment remain constant. Actual lenders may calculate interest daily, apply payments on different dates, charge fees, apply overpayments differently or change payments and terms. The lender's figures and mortgage documents take precedence.

Before you act

Check your mortgage offer and lender terms for overpayment allowances, early repayment charges and the effect of extra payments. Keep an appropriate emergency fund. For a recommendation about a mortgage or a regulated product decision, seek help from an appropriately authorised mortgage adviser. This prototype is not a substitute for professional advice.

Commercial relationships

If Morrow adds sponsored placements, referral links or other paid relationships, they should be clearly marked and explained. Commercial relationships must not alter calculator outputs or be represented as independent advice.
