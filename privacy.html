Privacy notes

Prototype notice: this page describes the current starter build and must be reviewed and tailored before a public commercial launch. It is not legal advice.

What is stored

The app stores mortgage and home-value figures in your browser's local storage so the plan persists between visits on the same browser. This build does not send those figures to a Morrow server. Anyone with access to your unlocked device/browser may be able to view a saved plan.

Backups

If you export a backup or CSV, you choose where the file is stored. Keep it private because it may reveal mortgage and property information. You can delete the locally saved plan in Settings.

Hosting and external resources

Your hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs. The current stylesheet loads fonts from Google Fonts when online, which may connect your browser to Google's servers. Remove that import if you want to avoid the third-party request.

Before launch

Publish the operator's legal identity and contact details, define retention/deletion processes, review hosting logs and vendors, assess whether the ICO data protection fee applies, and add consent controls before enabling non-essential analytics, advertising trackers or similar technologies.
