Mortgage basics, simply explained.

Useful ideas for understanding your numbers. This is general information, not personal financial advice.

%

What is LTV?

Loan-to-value is your mortgage balance divided by your home’s estimated value, as a percentage. If you owe £120,000 on a home worth £200,000, your LTV is 60%.

Example£120,000 ÷ £200,000 = 60% LTV
↘

Why do LTV bands matter?

Lenders often group mortgages into LTV bands. A lower band may offer different products, but income, credit history, fees, property type and lender criteria matter too.

RememberA target is not a guaranteed rate.
＋

What is an overpayment?

Money paid above your required monthly payment. It may reduce the balance faster, but rules and treatment depend on your lender and product.

Before paying extraCheck allowances, charges and emergency savings.
◷

What is a deal end date?

The date an introductory or fixed rate ends. It can prompt you to review options in good time; it is not the same as your full mortgage term ending.
